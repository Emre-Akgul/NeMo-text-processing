test_cases_sentences_fleurs.txt contains sentences from the Turkish (tr_tr)
transcriptions of FLEURS, used as written inputs, each with its spoken form.

FLEURS: Few-shot Learning Evaluation of Universal Representations of Speech.
Alexis Conneau, Min Ma, Simran Khanuja, Yu Zhang, Vera Axelrod, Siddharth Dalmia,
Jason Riesa, Clara Rivera, Ankur Bapna. 2022. https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12446
Data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/google/fleurs

The FLEURS data is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
The written sentences are used unchanged; the spoken forms are new and were
produced for this test set.
